About AVA Dental Clinic

AVA Dental Clinic — a single, independently-owned clinic in District 10, Ho Chi Minh City, specializing in dental implants, cosmetic porcelain crowns and orthodontics for local, overseas Vietnamese and international patients.

"As a physician, my first duty is to thank the generations of doctors before us, who left behind a treasury of medical knowledge for us to build on."

AVA Dental Clinic is one of the trusted names in dental care and cosmetic dentistry. With years of clinical experience, AVA has become a preferred choice for many patients in Ho Chi Minh City and across southern Vietnam — including a growing number of overseas Vietnamese and expats planning dental treatment alongside a trip home.

AVA's goal is always the patient's best outcome, so the clinic continually invests in modern equipment and up-to-date techniques across dental implants, cosmetic porcelain crowns, orthodontics, teeth whitening and general oral health treatment.

AVA Dental Clinic operates from a single location and is not part of any dental chain or larger cosmetic group.
