Professional summary

Who is Dr. Pham Viet Hung?

Dr. Pham Viet Hung is an Odonto-Stomatology specialist with deep clinical focus on dental implants and orthodontics. Holding an official practice certificate (No. 008280/HCM-CCHN) and trained at the University of Medicine and Pharmacy at Ho Chi Minh City as well as with international bodies (ICOI, ITI), Dr. Hung is committed to delivering safe, effective, medical-grade treatment for every patient at AVA Dental Clinic — including overseas Vietnamese and international patients travelling for dental care.

Education & degrees

Training and academic qualifications

  • Graduate, Odonto-Stomatology, University of Medicine and Pharmacy at Ho Chi Minh City.
  • Graduate, Specialist Level I in Odonto-Stomatology, University of Medicine and Pharmacy at Ho Chi Minh City.
  • Dental Implant certification from the University of Medicine and Pharmacy at HCMC and the Central Odonto-Stomatology Hospital, HCMC.
  • Basic and advanced orthodontics (braces) certifications.
